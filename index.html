About me

My background in psychology trained me to look for patterns in human behavior, and I bring that same mindset into analytics — asking better questions, digging into the “why,” and turning messy information into something clear.

Outside of work and school, you'll usually find me baking something new, climbing at my gym, or hanging out with my bird Zuko. I'm building toward a career where I can combine analytics, problem-solving, and user-focused thinking to help organizations make smarter, data-driven decisions.
